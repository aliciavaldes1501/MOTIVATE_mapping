### Dimensions of local plant diversity ################
"Cover" - joint summed cover of co-occurring taxa (in %)
"Species\nrichness" - number of co-occurring taxa
"Shannon\ndiversity" - richness and evenness of co-occurring taxa
"Functional\nrichness" - volume of the functional space occupied by the species within a community 
"Functional\nevenness" - abundance-weighted regularity of species distribution in multi-dimensional functional space
"Functional\ndivergence" - abundance-weighted mean divergence of co-occurring taxa in multi-dimensional trait space
"Faith\nphylogenetic diversity" - sum of all phylogenetic branch lengths
"Mean pairwise\nphylogenetic distance" - Mean phylogenetic branch length between all pairs of co-occurring taxa, weighted by abundance
"Mean nearest\ntaxon distance" - Mean nearest distance to the most closely related taxa, weighted by abundance
"No.of\nRed List species" - number of threatened species according to harmonized national and European Red Lists
"Cover of\nRed List species" - koint cover of threatened species according to harmonized national and European Red Lists
"No. of\nnon-native species" - number of non-native species, according to FloraVeg.EU database
"Cover of\nnon-native species" - number of non-native species, according to FloraVeg.EU database
"No. of\nspecialists" - number of species that were classified as 10% lowest Simpson-dissimilarity values among the plots in which they occurred
"Cover of\nspecialists" - joint cover of species that were classified as 10% lowest Simpson-dissimilarity values among the plots in which they occurred
"No. of\ngeneralists" - number of species that were classified as 10% highest Simpson-dissimilarity values among the plots in which they occurred
"Cover of\ngeneralists" - Joint cover of species that were classified as 10% highest Simpson-dissimilarity values among the plots in which they occurred
"Community-weighted\nmean niche width" - abundance-weighted mean value species-Level average Simpson-dissimilarity values

### Column explanations ################################

"metric" - type of biodiversity metric#
"time_seriesID" - combination of projectID, siteID, and plotID to identify individual time series
"slope" - average increase in biodiversity metric over one year (estimated with linear ordinary least square models)
"initial_div_value" - value of the diversity metric at the first Observation date
"EUNIS_start" - EUNIS habitat classification at the first observation date
"EUNIS_end" - EUNIS habitat classification at the last observation date
"year_start" - year of the first obsevation
"year_end" - year of the last observation
"n" - number of observations
"Trajectory" - expert-assigned change in habitat type (stable, succession, disturbance, other, unclassifiable
"plot_size" - area of vegetation plot (in m²)
